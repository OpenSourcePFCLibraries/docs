# Page Setup dialog box

Use this dialog box to control the paper size and source, the orientation, and the margins.

*Opened by `w_pagesetup`.*

## Options

- **Paper size**: The paper size for your printer.
- **Paper source**: The tray from which the pages are drawn.
- **Orientation**: Portrait (the short edge of the paper is horizontal) or landscape (the short edge of the paper is vertical).
- **Margins**: The distance between the edges of the paper and the top, bottom, left, and right edges of the text.
